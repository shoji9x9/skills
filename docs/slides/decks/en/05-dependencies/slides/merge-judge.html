Deciding whether to merge

What to check depends on the update type. If CI fails, nothing is merged.

Update typeWhat to checkHandling
Minor or patch, 1.0 and laterIs the changelog mostly bug fixes and small improvements?Relatively safe
Minor or patch, 0.xAre APIs changed or removed, or does behavior change?If unclear, check before merging
majorDo the listed breaking changes affect the code?If so, do not merge; write the needed fixes on the PR

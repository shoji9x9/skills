pr-finalize-loop

Repeats fixes and review handling until CI passes and no findings remain open.

  • One round: fix CI failures, reply to and resolve findings, commit and push, request a re-review
  • You choose the re-reviewer (Copilot, Claude Code, Codex, local, none)
  • Always stops at the round limit (default 5). With local /code-review only, it then lowers effort and adds half the rounds (e.g. high 5 → medium 2 → low 1)
  • Asks the user only about findings that need a person

How to invoke

pr-finalize-loop <PR URL>

Each round handles reviews the same way as pr-review-handle

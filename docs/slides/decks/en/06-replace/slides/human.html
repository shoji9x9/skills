Where people decide

SkillWhen a person decides
replace-strategyYou approve the strategy, and decide how to sort the current app's weak points, the static asset policy, and which issues to file
current-environment-bootstrapThe current app's owners and domain experts answer the questions
parity-suiteYou approve the items left unmeasured and the record of writes that cannot be cleaned up
parity-replaceYou approve dropping acceptance criteria and the assets left unchecked
parity-diffYou approve differences classified as "acceptable" once per cause

People decide the new app's architecture (framework, structure, and so on) in advance; the skills do not. With --autonomous, the agent records items that need a decision as pending and asks about them together at the end.

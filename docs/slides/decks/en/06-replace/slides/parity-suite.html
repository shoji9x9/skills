parity-suite

Builds pass/fail criteria with Playwright against the current app, usable on both apps.

  • Elements are referenced by logical name, so the new app only needs a mapping to reuse the criteria
  • Records and replays API responses to pin down behavior
  • Injects faults on purpose to confirm the criteria are strong enough to catch them
  • Captures visual baselines and noise ranges, and hands them to parity-diff

How to invoke

parity-suite --feature <slug>

Handles one feature per run. Does not touch the new app

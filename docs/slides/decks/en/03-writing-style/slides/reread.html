Rereading summary lines

Before publishing headings, issue and PR titles and the first line of a commit message, reread them as someone who has not seen the body, with 3 questions.

1

Can the object be read as belonging to a verb before the real predicate?

2

Does the sentence say what each noun phrase refers to (remainder of what, which target)?

3

Does the sentence say what each general term refers to (command, syntax, file)?

Learning from failures

kaizen extracts learnings from failures and applies them to skills, rules, and Hooks.

Fix the root cause

It analyzes why a failure happened and fixes that cause, instead of patching the single failure.

Prefer deterministic checks

It prefers linters, Hooks, and scripts as targets. It does not rely only on instructions in rules or docs.

Share across agents

Learnings live in .kaizen/, and every agent reads them.

Diagram rules

The agent always checks the diagram rules when it writes the spec or fixes a diagram.

AspectRule
CrossingsMinimizing crossings comes first. A remaining crossing gets a jump mark to show the lines do not connect
Line shapeLines are horizontal or vertical only. The engine stops with an error on diagonal lines
AlignmentNodes in the same layer share x or y and sit on a grid
LabelsNode labels go on a side with no lines. Line labels avoid bend points
SpacingParallel lines are at least 20px apart. The canvas has at least 50px of margin

Safety rules

Per-environment configuration and human approval decide which operations may run.

OperationHandling
No side effectsRuns without approval (navigating, typing a search, switching tabs, choosing a filter, and so on)
Operations with side effectsFor operations that write, charge, create, or notify ("Save", "Delete", "Send"), the skill explains what and why, and runs them after approval
Forbidden operationsOperations matching the environment's forbidden_actions do not run. The skill asks the user to do them
Unconfigured environmentTreated as read-only

A local environment may still use a shared DB or external APIs. Unless the skill can confirm they are separate, it assumes the shared environment is affected and asks for approval.
